# PRC Market and Governance Adaptation

Use this reference when the review involves a PRC fund, domestic securities firm, onshore data, non-listed equity, RMB reporting, performance undertakings, or local approval/reporting.

## Authority and Date Discipline

- Identify the entity type, accounting basis, fund documents, valuation policy, regulator/SRO context, and reporting purpose before applying a rule.
- Retrieve the current official source for any regulatory or association requirement used in the conclusion. Record title, issuing body, effective date, accessed version, and applicable clause; do not rely on a remembered rule name.
- Separate accounting fair value, fund-policy valuation, IC decision value, transaction price, and regulatory/reporting value. They may legitimately differ.

## Fair-Value and Instrument Context

- Record the stated fair-value hierarchy level and evidence supporting it. Do not assign Level 1/2/3 solely from an issuer being listed or unlisted.
- For non-listed equity, capture the latest financing date and terms, comparable set, liquidity/marketability adjustment, control/minority basis, instrument seniority, liquidation preference, conversion, anti-dilution, redemption, and other rights that affect allocation.
- When an option-pricing, probability-weighted, backsolve, Black-Scholes, Monte Carlo, or hybrid method is used, test the scenario set, volatility, time, discount rate, correlations, allocation mechanics, and calibration date.
- Treat 对赌/业绩承诺, 回购, 赎回, 优先清算, 转股, 反稀释, and control rights as contractual value drivers, not narrative footnotes.

## Domestic Data

- Prefer an approved Wind/Choice/internal adapter when configured. Record vendor field, security identifier, pull time, market date, and adjustment basis.
- Reconcile A-share/H-share/overseas listings, RMB/foreign currency, trading calendar, fiscal year, tax basis, share classes, and corporate actions before comparing values.
- For missing private-market evidence, preserve the gap and lower the evidence-confidence assessment; do not synthesize a precise mark from weak proxies.

## Fund Terms and Approvals

- Translate carry, hurdle/preferred return, catch-up, clawback, escrow, management fee, and allocation language from the governing fund documents into explicit formulas before calculating a waterfall.
- Distinguish fund-level and deal-by-deal waterfalls and label tax, FX, recycling, recallable distributions, and fee offsets.
- Record required internal review gates such as valuation function, IC, risk, compliance, finance, IR, valuation committee, or external valuer according to the actual organization. Do not infer sign-off from a staged report.

## Minimum Localized Output Fields

Include Chinese instrument/entity names, valuation date, report date, source/as-of date, currency and unit, accounting/policy basis, fair-value hierarchy where required, method and weight authority, data vendor fields, material contractual rights, approval status, and open evidence gaps.

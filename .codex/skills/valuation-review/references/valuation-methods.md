# Valuation Methods and Reconciliation

Use the applicable sections only. Policy, mandate, instrument terms, and source evidence override screening heuristics.

## DCF

Recompute or challenge:

1. Revenue, margins, tax, D&A, CapEx, and working-capital drivers against historical performance and cited operating evidence.
2. Free-cash-flow definition and its consistency with the discount rate.
3. Risk-free rate, beta, equity risk premium, cost of debt, tax rate, capital structure, and country/size/other premiums, each with source date and unit.
4. Explicit forecast discount factors and convention.
5. Perpetuity-growth and/or exit-multiple terminal value, including discount-back period.
6. Enterprise-to-equity bridge and diluted per-share value.
7. WACC-by-terminal-growth sensitivity, plus operating scenarios where the conclusion is assumption-sensitive.

Report terminal value as a share of enterprise value. `>75%` is a default attention flag supplied by this workflow, not a universal policy breach; interpret it in light of company maturity, forecast length, and approved policy.

## Trading Comparables

- Document selection criteria, inclusion/exclusion decisions, geography, sector, business model, size, growth, margins, leverage, liquidity, and data date.
- Keep enterprise-value multiples separate from equity-value multiples. Align numerators and denominators.
- Label LTM, NTM, calendarized, and fiscal-period measures explicitly.
- Normalize one-offs only with a traceable adjustment bridge.
- Show the full distribution and the effect of exclusions. Prefer median or another policy-approved statistic; do not remove an outlier merely because it weakens the desired conclusion.
- Reconcile selected multiple to the subject company's growth, margin, risk, scale, and control/liquidity characteristics.

## Precedent Transactions

- Record announcement date, closing date, target condition, consideration, stake acquired, control status, geography, cycle, and transaction-specific adjustments.
- Use metrics known at the relevant transaction date and align periods with the subject company.
- Separate strategic synergies, control, scarcity, and cycle effects from stand-alone value.
- A `20%-40%` control-premium range may be used only as a screening reference when no governing policy is available; never apply it automatically or substitute it for transaction evidence.

## Valuation Reconciliation

Build a bridge that shows, for every method:

- low / base / high value
- enterprise or equity basis
- valuation date, currency, unit, and per-share denominator
- reported value and independently recomputed value
- method weight, source of the weight, and weighted contribution
- key limitations and evidence quality

Show unweighted ranges first. If the applicable policy is silent, do not silently default to a weighted point. The following are review heuristics only: DCF `40%-60%`, trading comps `25%-40%`, precedents `15%-25%`. Any selected weights remain a named human/policy decision and must sum to 100%.

## Reasonableness Checks

At minimum, test:

1. implied valuation multiple against the subject's history
2. premium/discount against appropriately matched peers
3. implied revenue, margin, and long-run growth embedded in the selected value
4. implied investor IRR or MOIC from known entry/current/exit assumptions
5. equity value and per-share conclusion against observable market capitalization or latest financing, with basis differences reconciled

Also test terminal-value dependence, forecast hockey-stick behavior, scenario monotonicity, and whether downside cases actually incorporate downside assumptions.

## PE Returns and Waterfall

When relevant, recompute gross and net MOIC/IRR using dated cash flows and distinguish realized, unrealized, and total value. Test sensitivities across:

- entry multiple by exit multiple
- EBITDA/revenue growth by exit multiple
- leverage by exit multiple
- holding period by exit multiple
- bull/base/bear operating cases

Attribute value creation separately to operating growth, multiple change, leverage/deleveraging, fees, FX, and residual. For fund waterfalls, tie beginning NAV, contributions, distributions, fees/expenses, realized/unrealized change, carry, clawback/escrow, and ending NAV before allocating LP/GP amounts. Never hide a residual in carry or an unexplained plug.
